PATIENT EDUCATION

The ABCDE Rule of Melanoma Detection

Dermatologists use this evidence-based clinical framework to screen atypical moles. If you spot any of these signs, seek a medical evaluation:

A

Asymmetry

One half of the spot does not match the other half in shape or contour.

B

Border

The edges are irregular, ragged, notched, blurred, or poorly defined.

C

Color

The color is uneven, with varying shades of tan, brown, black, red, or white.

D

Diameter

The lesion is greater than 6 millimeters across (about the size of a pencil eraser).

E

Evolving

The mole is changing in size, shape, color, or symptoms (itching or bleeding).
