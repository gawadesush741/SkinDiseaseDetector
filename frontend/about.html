01 · ARCHITECTURE

Deep Residual Learning (ResNet-18)

The classification backbone utilizes a ResNet-18 convolutional neural network pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned for dermatological dermoscopy. The residual skip connections prevent vanishing gradient problems, allowing the network to retain fine-grained micro-features (pigment network reticulation, globules, and atypical vascular structures).

Input Dimensions: 224 × 224 × 3 RGB
Loss Function: Weighted Cross-Entropy
Inference Engine: PyTorch CPU Execution
Model Parameters: ~11.2 Million
02 · DATASET

HAM10000 & ISIC Benchmark

The training distribution incorporates verified dermoscopy cases from the International Skin Imaging Collaboration (ISIC) and HAM10000 ("Human Against Machine with 10,000 training images") collection from the Medical University of Vienna and Cliff Rosendahl in Queensland, Australia.

  • Nevus (nv): Common benign melanocytic mole
  • Melanoma (mel): Invasive malignant melanocytic tumor
  • Basal Cell Carcinoma (bcc): Common non-melanoma skin cancer
  • Squamous Cell Carcinoma (scc): Keratinocyte malignancy
  • Actinic Keratoses (akiec): Solar UV precancerous lesion
  • Pigmented Benign Keratosis (bkl): Seborrheic keratosis / solar lentigo
  • Dermatofibroma (df): Benign histiocytic skin growth
03 · DIVERSITY & BIAS

Fitzpatrick Skin Phototype Statement

Standard historical dermoscopy benchmarks (including HAM10000) feature an over-representation of fair skin tones (Fitzpatrick phototypes I–III). Accuracy may decrease when assessing pigmented lesions in darker skin tones (types IV–VI), such as acral lentiginous melanoma on palms and soles.

Recommendation: For patients with skin phototypes IV–VI, algorithmic predictions must be verified by a clinician specialized in ethnic dermatology.

04 · REGULATORY POSTURE

Educational Prototype Disclaimer

Dermalens AI is distributed solely for research and educational demonstrations. It is not an FDA-cleared, CE-marked, or MHRA-approved diagnostic medical device. It should never be used as a primary diagnostic instrument, nor should medical interventions or biopsies be initiated or withheld based solely on algorithmic output.
